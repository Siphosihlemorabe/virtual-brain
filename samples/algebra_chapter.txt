Chapter 4: Algebra (Grade 10)

Concept C1: Solving algebraic equations
To solve an equation, first collect like terms: 3x + 2x = 5x, because you add the numbers in front of x. Then do the opposite operation to both sides to get x alone.

Problem P1 (C1): Solve 3x + 2x = 20.
Worked solution:
coefficient of x = 3 + 2 = 5
x = 20/5 = 4

Problem P2 (C1): Solve x + 7 = 12.
Worked solution:
x = 12 - 7 = 5
