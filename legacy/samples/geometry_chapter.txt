Chapter 7: Euclidean geometry (Grade 10)

Concept C1: Euclidean geometry
Angle facts: the angles in a triangle add up to 180 degrees, and angles on a straight line add up to 180 degrees. Many problems need algebra to find the unknown.

Problem P1 (C1): The angles of a triangle are 2x, 3x and 4x. Find x.
Worked solution:
angle sum = 180 (uses: Euclidean geometry)
coefficient of x = 2 + 3 + 4 = 9 (uses: Solving algebraic equations)
x = 180/9 = 20 (uses: Solving algebraic equations)

Problem P2 (C1): Two angles lie on a straight line. One is 110 degrees. Find the other.
Worked solution:
angles on a straight line = 180 (uses: Euclidean geometry)
other angle = 180 - 110 = 70 (uses: Euclidean geometry)
